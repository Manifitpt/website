About me

Hi, my name is Amal. I’m an internationally certified personal trainer with a passion for transforming lives through fitness. Driven by a personal journey of self-discovery through training, I found my calling in helping others realize their untapped potential.

From guiding weight loss journeys to creating personalized strength training, my experience extends to flexibility, stretching, posture improvement and addressing muscle imbalances. I believe a well-rounded fitness routine not only enhances physical performance, but also contributes to overall well-being.
